## Where the pick has fewer ticks

The score compares products; it does not choose. Where a runner-up reaches more routes than the pick, the pick's reason is shown beside it.

| Element | Pick (access) | Runner-up (access) | Why the pick stands |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text to speech | [Gemini API (Google)](https://ai.google.dev) (75) | [Cartesia](https://cartesia.ai) (100) | The two are tied on quality and Gemini costs a third as much; on a phone call, the delay before the first word decides. ElevenLabs is the runner-up and the most adopted (2.1 million Python downloads a week, every agent route), but its best model ranks twelfth on the voice arena and costs $50 per million characters, against Gemini's $16.50. Cartesia is the easier of the two picks for an agent to drive (a hosted MCP server, a CLI and skills), so the test call decides between them. |

LiveKit documents Telnyx trunks step by step. ElevenLabs Agents is the runner-up; Vapi is the quickest managed path at $0.05 a minute. |
| Model gateway | [Vercel AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway) (75) | [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) (95) | It carries Jev and open models; a gateway owned by a payments company is a concentration risk, and a retired model is swapped here by configuration and re-verified. OpenRouter is the runner-up and the more agent-native (a hosted MCP server, a CLI, 19 skills, per-agent keys by API) and the most used (8 million users, 100 trillion tokens a month), but it charges 5.5% on credit and Stripe has agreed to buy it; Vercel charges no markup or platform fee, even when the entity brings its own keys. |
